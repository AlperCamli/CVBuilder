A few quick answers.

Can I start without a card?

You can complete onboarding before entering card details. Existing users can view and manually edit saved CVs for free. Creating a new CV, exporting, AI tools, and tailoring require an active trial or Pro subscription. The 3-day trial requires a card and renews weekly unless cancelled before it ends.

How is the Pro trial different?

Eligible users can try Weekly Pro free for 3 days. A card is required at checkout. It then renews at the displayed regional Weekly price unless cancelled before the trial ends. The trial applies to Weekly, not Monthly. Checkout confirms eligibility and terms.

Can I review the changes?

Always. Select the keywords that fit, review suggestions, and edit your CV before exporting. Your experience stays the source of truth.
