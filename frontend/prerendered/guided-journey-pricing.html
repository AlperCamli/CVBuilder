A few quick answers.

Can I look around before adding a card?

Yes. You can answer a few quick questions and see your CV take shape without entering any card details. When you’re ready to create, tailor and download, start your 3-day free trial: you add a card at secure Stripe checkout, pay nothing today and can cancel anytime. If you don’t cancel before the trial ends, Weekly Pro renews weekly at the price shown. Already have an account? You can always view and edit your saved CVs for free.

Why do you ask for a card for the free trial?

It keeps the trial fair for everyone and lets you carry on with Pro without interruption if you love it. You won’t be charged during the 3 days, and payments are handled securely by Stripe — we never see or store your card number. Cancel from your account before the trial ends and you won’t pay anything. The trial is for Weekly Pro and eligible users; checkout confirms your eligibility and terms.

Can I review the changes?

Always. You choose the keywords that fit, review every suggestion and edit your CV before you download it. Your real experience always comes first.
