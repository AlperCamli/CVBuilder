A few quick answers.

Can I start without a card?

Yes. The Free plan includes 3 tailored CVs, 5 exports, and 20 AI actions per month. Each allowance has its own limit. No credit card is required.

How is the Pro trial different?

Eligible new subscribers can try Weekly Pro free for 3 days. A card is required at checkout. It then renews at $4.99 per week unless cancelled before the trial ends. The trial applies to Weekly, not Monthly. Checkout confirms eligibility and terms.

Can I review the changes?

Always. Select the keywords that fit, review suggestions, and edit your CV before exporting. Your experience stays the source of truth.
